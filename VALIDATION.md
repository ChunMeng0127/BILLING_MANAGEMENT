## Worker assignment workflow and weekly updates

WorkerAssignment responsibility begins on the Monday of the assignment-created week and is evaluated from workflow/visibility history, independent of BillingRecord service dates. Workflow Status is part of the Weekly Update: WorkerAssignment stores the current state while WeeklyProgressReport keeps the historical snapshot submitted for its week. The completion week remains required; a batch-completed, visible, uncancelled assignment with no current-week report may submit exactly one final report whose status remains Completed. Later Completed, Hidden and Cancelled weeks stop responsibility, and Hidden/Cancelled assignments cannot use the final-report exception. Reopen and Unhide resume responsibility from the current transition week without rewriting earlier Missing/Submitted/Late results. Batch workflow updates change current operational state/history only and do not create WeeklyProgressReports. Reporting deadlines use configurable `BusinessTimeZone` (default Asia/Kuala_Lumpur); Missing is calculated, Submitted is on time and Late is after Sunday. A worker’s current-week report edit updates the assignment’s current status/version/progress and appends history while preserving report SubmittedAt/CreatedAt/CreatedBy; stale report or assignment versions are rejected. Staff correct existing reports only, and assignment worker identity remains locked once any report exists.
